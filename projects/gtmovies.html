GT Movies Store

An online movie shop in Django 5. I typed every line myself instead of copying the book’s repository, then added a feature the book never covers.

The part that was mine

The book has no way to deal with a bad review. I designed and built one. Anyone can report a review, and it leaves public view right away.

By the middle chapters I could guess the next step before reading it. Writing a feature with no instructions was how I checked that the framework had actually sunk in.

The shop

Browse and search the catalog, read and write reviews, fill a cart, check out, and look back at past orders. Admins manage users, movies and reviews from the Django admin.
