What it is

PandaPulse connects job seekers with employers through structured profiles, job listings and an application pipeline. It serves three kinds of user, job seekers, recruiters and administrators, each with their own tools.

It placed 3rd out of every submission across both class sections, which exempted our team from the final exam. Beyond the 15 required user stories we designed and built more than 20 features of our own.

For job seekers

Seekers search and filter jobs, apply with a note and a résumé that gets parsed into their profile, book interviews from the employer’s open slots, message employers, and compare offers side by side with their own weights for salary, location, visa sponsorship and benefits.

For recruiters and admins

Recruiters post jobs, move applicants across a kanban pipeline, compare candidates, save searches that alert them to new matches, archive candidates for later, and see where applicants live on a clustered map.

What we added on our own

None of these were in the assignment.

  • Panda Paul, the chatbot mascotSigned-in users can move around the site, update their profile, apply to jobs and message people by talking to it.

  • Response-time badgeEach employer shows how many days they usually take to reply, right on the listing.
  • Résumé parserFills profile fields from an uploaded résumé and normalizes skills so matching works better.
  • Viewed indicatorApplicants see the moment an employer opens their application.
  • Applicant comparisonRecruiters compare several applicants for a role and re-rank them against their priorities.
  • Saved search alertsRecruiters save a candidate search and hear when new matches appear.
  • Company culture pagesCulture, perks and an office map so candidates can judge the fit before applying.
  • Dark modeA site-wide toggle saved per user.
  • Skill endorsementsEmployers endorse skills after an interview, adding proof to a profile.
  • One-click follow-upAfter a set number of days, applicants send a polite follow-up from a template.
  • Rejection feedback templatesStructured rejections with an optional personal note, so every candidate hears something real.
  • Applicant archiveRejected candidates are kept so they can be revisited when a new role opens.
  • Interview feedbackEmployers log structured notes and the reason for each decision after an interview.
  • Confetti on submitSending an application gets a small celebration.
  • Account recoveryForgot-username and forgot-password flows that email a recovery link.

How it was built

Everything is Django, using its ORM, class-based views and templates throughout. The course is about object-oriented design, so we kept the models cleanly separated and the components reusable.

The maps use Google Maps to pin job locations and cluster applicants by area. Pulses, Panda Paul and the offer comparison tool were built by our team with no starter code. Dark mode is a preference saved per user in the database and applied by switching a class on the page’s root element.
