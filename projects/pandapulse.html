PandaPulse

A hiring site in the spirit of LinkedIn, built from scratch in Django by our team for CS 2340.

Read the code on GitHub
When
March 2026
Course
CS 2340, Objects and Design
Built with
Django, Python, SQLite, JavaScript
Scope
15 required features and more than 20 of our own
Result
3rd of all teams across both sections, which exempted us from the final exam

For job seekers

Seekers search and filter jobs, apply with a note and a résumé that gets parsed into their profile, book interviews from the employer’s open slots, and compare offers side by side with their own weights for salary, location, visa sponsorship and benefits.

For recruiters and admins

Recruiters post jobs, move applicants across a kanban pipeline, compare candidates, save searches that alert them to new matches, and see where applicants live on a clustered map.

What we added on our own

None of these were in the assignment.

  • Panda PaulA chatbot that can update your profile, apply to jobs and send messages for you.
  • PulsesShort videos people and companies post to promote themselves.
  • Offer comparisonWeighted side-by-side comparison of job offers.
  • Interview calendarSeekers pick from slots the employer sets.
  • Response-time badgeEach employer shows how many days they usually take to reply.
  • Résumé parserFills profile fields from an uploaded résumé and normalizes skills.
  • Viewed indicatorApplicants see when an employer opens their application.
  • Saved search alertsRecruiters hear about new matching candidates.
  • Skill endorsementsEmployers endorse skills after an interview.
  • Rejection templatesEvery rejected candidate gets real feedback.
  • Applicant archiveRejected candidates can be revisited when a new role opens.
  • Dark modeSaved per user in the database.
